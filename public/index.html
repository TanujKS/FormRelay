DEV_MODE

When DEV_MODE is enabled in your Worker environment, all submissions are sent only to defaultNotifyTo from config/defaults.json, ignoring any per-form notifyTo settings.

Configure in wrangler.toml (or via the dashboard) with a value of "true", "1", or "yes".
